# Supervised-pilot inventory — what exists, and in what sense

Issue #18 (2026-11-02) item 4. Baseline `f6f979d`, CI 6/6, reconfirmed before writing.

**Three labels, and the distinction between them is the whole document.**

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| **EXECUTED** | The real production code ran and wrote the real record. A failure here would be a product bug. |
| **OBSERVED** | NH read a fact out of supplied data and reported it. The reading is real; the fact's truth rests on the supplier. |
| **SIMULATED** | NH, or its harness, produced the input or the event. Nothing outside NH asserted it. |
| **NOT IMPLEMENTED** | No code path exists. |

A stage can be EXECUTED and SIMULATED at once — the arc below is full of those, and saying only the
first half is how a demo gets mistaken for a pilot.

---

## 1 · The arc Issue #18 names

| # | Stage | Status | What is actually true |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Upload** | **EXECUTED** · data SIMULATED | A real CSV goes through the real upload screen → API → admission → `pilot_dataset_submissions` (journey §3, §8). The *bytes* are generated by the harness; no customer file has ever been uploaded. |
| 2 | **Detection** | **EXECUTED** · input SIMULATED | A real worker claims a real task and the real detector runs (journey §5 for assessment, §13 for the case arc, §14 for reconciliation). In §13 the observation is **injected** by `scripts/seed-journey-activation-task.ts`, because NH has **no screen that creates a finding** — deliberately: a UI that let an operator hand-write one would let the beneficiary author what the review gate exists to judge. |
| 3 | **Candidate** | **EXECUTED** | Written by the production publisher to `agent_case_candidates` with its real dedupe key; the queue read is the real API. |
| 4 | **Review** | **EXECUTED** | Operator accept/reject through the screen, immutable review row, and a decision with no reason is refused by the screen (§13a). Accept and promote are two separate acts (§13b). |
| 5 | **Case opened** | **EXECUTED** | `RecoveryCaseRecord` root, `sourceCandidateId` UNIQUE, a second case for one finding refused by two independent mechanisms (§13c). |
| 6 | **Proposed correction** | **NOT IMPLEMENTED** | `BillingCorrection` is a `RecoveryReason` only — a label a proof carries. There is no request, approval, execution or status. Specified in `BILLING_CORRECTION_EXECUTION_SPEC_V1.md`, blocked on the S-DUP-4 precedence decision. |
| 6b | **"Corrective action recorded"** | **EXECUTED as a RECORD** | The journey's *Record corrective action* step writes a governed intervention event and the case reads INTERVENED. **No billing system was contacted and no charge was raised.** The record says an action was taken; NH did not take it. |
| 7 | **External settlement evidence** | **EXECUTED verification** · evidence SIMULATED | Ed25519 verification is the real production path and really refuses tampering (§14b refuses evidence altered after signing). But the signing key is **generated per run** by the harness, so the "source system" is NH's own fixture. No customer system has ever signed anything. The readiness ladder says the same thing from the other end: nothing reaches `AUTHORITY_VERIFIED`, ceiling `SOURCE_NATIVE`, every level PROVISIONAL. |
| 8 | **PROVEN / refusal** | **EXECUTED** | `Collected − Baseline` from a locked baseline and referenced evidence; separation of duties enforced; the obligation-identity gate refuses a claim whose subject is unknown or aggregate, with nine negative controls. The arc ends with **$100.00 identified beside $70.00 proven** — two numbers on one screen, neither standing in for the other. |

## 2 · So what is a pilot missing

Three things, and only the third is work I could do without a decision.

1. **A real customer file.** Every stage above has run only on data NH generated. This is a business
   step, not a code step: the intake package exists (`docs/pilot-intake/`, 10 artefacts generated from the
   governed field specs) and nothing has been sent.
2. **A real signing source.** Until a customer system signs a settlement record, stage 7's evidence is
   NH's fixture signed by NH's key. `AUTHORITY_VERIFIED` needs one of: a signed export, a fetch NH
   performs itself, a machine-issued system-of-record attestation, or third-party reconciliation.
3. **Correction visibility in the product.** Closed in this slice: the correction ledger now has a
   screen (`src/modules/CorrectionLedger.tsx`), `AuditRead`-gated, showing every observed amount, the
   stored figure, the refusal states, and the two undecided magnitudes **never netted**.

## 3 · What no amount of further synthetic work can establish

That a customer can supply the facts; that a billing correction was issued; that money was collected.
The first needs a file, the second needs the unapproved mechanism, the third needs an independent source.
**No queue status, green CI, candidate row, read-only report or screen is evidence of any of them**, and
nothing in this repository claims otherwise.
